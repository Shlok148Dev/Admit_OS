## App Description (Full Description)

**Welcome to ADMIT OS — the post-exam operating system for Indian students.**

Navigating JEE, NEET, MHT-CET, KCET, or 20+ other competitive exam admissions? The moment your exam is over, a completely new universe of anxiety begins. Marks App owned your prep; ADMIT OS owns your admission journey. 

We are your command center from result day to college registration, helping you make the most important decisions of your life with hyper-accurate, verified data and zero stress.

### Why Students Trust ADMIT OS:
1. **Zero Hallucinations, 100% Sourced Cutoffs**: Every prediction, rank, and seat matrix cutoff is backed by official sources. No guesses, no bias, and no sponsored colleges.
2. **Hyper-Accurate Predictor (Rank Radar)**: Input your rank, category (General, OBC-NCL, SC, ST, EWS, PwD), and state. Instantly see predicted opening and closing ranks with 90% confidence intervals and historical trend lines.
3. **AI Choice Filling Assistant (Counseling Compass)**: Build your optimized choice list step-by-step. Get clear explanations for every suggested option based on your preferences (branch brand vs location vs fees).
4. **Branch vs Brand Decision Maker (Branch Compass)**: Solve the age-old dilemma. Get actual placement statistics, 5-year salary trajectories, and career graphs for various disciplines.
5. **Real-time Deadline & Notification Nerve Center**: Never miss a round deadline, fee payment window, or document upload verification date. We monitor official portals 24/7 so you don't have to.

### Exams & Counseling Covered:
- **Engineering (National & State)**: JEE Main, JEE Advanced, BITSAT, MHT-CET, KCET, COMEDK, WBJEE, VITEEE, SRMJEEE, MET, and more.
- **Medical**: NEET-UG, NEET-PG.
- **Counseling Bodies**: JoSAA, CSAB, MCC (NEET), DTE Maharashtra, KEA Karnataka, JAC Delhi, and state counseling portals.

### Our Privacy and Trust Moat:
- **DPDP Act 2023 Compliant**: We respect your privacy. No personal student PII is exposed, and all data is local to India (GCP Mumbai).
- **No Sponsored Placements**: We do not accept referral fees or advertisements from colleges. Our recommendations are pure math and historical truth.

Get the peace of mind you deserve. Make your rank count with ADMIT OS.

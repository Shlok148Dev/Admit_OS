# ADMIT OS — Privacy Policy
**Last Updated: June 4, 2026**

ADMIT OS ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy is formulated in strict compliance with the **Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act 2023)** of India.

---

## 1. Core Principles of Data Protection

1. **Consent-Based Processing**: We process personal data only when we have received your explicit, unambiguous, and revocable consent.
2. **Data Minimization**: We collect only the minimum necessary data points required to deliver college predictions and choice list optimization.
3. **Purpose Limitation**: Your exam data is used strictly for prediction and counseling guidance. It is never sold, shared, or leased for advertisement or marketing purposes.
4. **Data Separation & Security**: Student PII (name, email, phone number) is stored in a separate, encrypted `users` database table. Technical exam details (ranks, categories, states) are stored separately in the `student_profiles` database table, referencing user records only via an anonymized database key.
5. **DPDP Compliance - No PII in Logs**: All application logs are automatically sanitized to prevent any PII or sensitive parameters from being captured in system diagnostics or audit trails.
6. **Data Localization**: All student personal data is hosted, processed, and stored on secure servers located within the Republic of India (Google Cloud Platform, Mumbai region - `asia-south1`).

---

## 2. Personal Data We Collect

To provide our core service, we collect:
- **Identity Information**: Name, email address, and phone number (for authentication purposes only, stored in the `users` table).
- **Exam Profile Information**: Primary exam (e.g., JEE Main, Advanced, NEET, MHT-CET, KCET), rank (CRL and Category Ranks), percentile, quota state, category, and gender (stored in the `student_profiles` table).
- **User Preference Weights**: Sliders indicating branch vs. college brand priority (stored in the `student_profiles` table).
- **Log Data**: Anonymized IP addresses, browser types, and application performance metrics.

---

## 3. How We Process and Use Personal Data

We process your data strictly to:
- Authenticate your account and prevent unauthorized access.
- Execute statistical models predicting your likelihood of admission to specific colleges.
- Order and optimize your choice-filling preferences.
- Notify you of critical counseling dates, deadlines, and seat allotments via Push, Email, and WhatsApp.

---

## 4. Rights of the Data Principal (Your Rights)

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you hold the following rights:
- **Right to Information**: You have the right to request a summary of the personal data we process and the processing activities.
- **Right to Correction and Erasure**: You have the right to correct inaccurate data or request the permanent deletion of your profile.
- **Right to Erasure SLA**: Upon requesting account deletion via the in-app settings or support, all your personal data (identity, exam details, and prediction history) will be permanently and irreversibly purged from our active databases and backup servers within **72 hours**.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent**: You can withdraw your consent to data processing at any time. Withdrawal will immediately stop active prediction services and lead to profile erasure.
- **Right to Grievance Redressal**: If you have concerns regarding data processing, you may contact our Grievance Officer at privacy@admitos.in.

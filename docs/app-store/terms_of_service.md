## 1. Description of Services

ADMIT OS is an educational guidance platform designed to assist students during the post-competitive exam counseling and admission process in India. The Platform provides tools such as:
- **Rank Radar**: Predicts possible college and branch allotments based on historical data.
- **Counseling Compass**: Generates optimized choice-filling sequences based on user-defined preferences.
